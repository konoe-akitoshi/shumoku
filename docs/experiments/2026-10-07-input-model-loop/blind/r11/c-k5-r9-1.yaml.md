1. **Not expressible:** The VMs’ virtual adapters and their association with the VLAN 10 and VLAN 30 port groups are not represented as separate interfaces. The format can show that the VMs are members of those segments, but it cannot say that membership is through a virtual adapter or port group. The cluster’s failover behavior (“VMs restart on another host”) is also not captured by the redundancy set alone.

2. **Guesses or additions:** I treated “server room” as a group containing the hosts and VMs. I used product path `vmware/esxi` and node types `switch`, `virtualization-host`, and `virtual-machine`; those labels are not specified by the description. I marked `mon-1` as assumed because it is known only from monitoring. Its group and VM type are also inferred from the company context and description.

3. **Unclear:** `Redundancy.nodes` says nodes stand in for one another, but does not explain how to represent a cluster whose members host workloads that restart on one another. Also, segment membership can be inferred from a link carrying the segment, but the format does not say directly how to record a VM’s membership when its virtual adapter is known but its link is not modeled.
