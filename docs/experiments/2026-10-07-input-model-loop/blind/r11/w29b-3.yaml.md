1. The format cannot express that AS64510 is our transit provider and we are its customer, or that AS64520 peering is settlement-free. Those are recorded as descriptions where applicable.
2. I added placeholder nodes for unnamed routers in the external ASes and inferred that br2 and the AS64520/64530 routers attach to the IX-T LAN. The description gives AS64520 and AS64530 LAN addresses but does not explicitly say their routers or physical attachments. I represented the AS64540 session as virtual because its cable or path is unknown; the format does not provide a separate “path unknown” marker.
3. It is unclear whether a link endpoint at a segment means a node is attached to that shared LAN, and whether a node-to-node virtual link can describe a logical session carried over a separately modeled segment attachment. The format also has no explicit representation for an AS-level eBGP session when the peer router is unnamed.
