1. The format has no explicit field for the commercial relationship “customer” or “transit,” or for settlement-free peering as a relationship type. I described those in labels/descriptions.
2. I inferred that each AS has a routing domain and that the IX peering LAN belongs to AS64500’s routing domain. I also added assumed router nodes for AS64520 and AS64530 to associate their stated addresses with nodes. The AS64540 peer’s identity and address were not given, so the endpoint is referenced but no node is defined.

It is unclear whether `assumed: true` applies to the unknown-name AS64510 router: the router is known to exist, but its identity/name is unknown, while the type comment says assumed means existence is unconfirmed. Also, a link to an undefined node ID may be invalid, and the format does not specify a way to represent an eBGP session between addresses as distinct from its underlying cable/path.
