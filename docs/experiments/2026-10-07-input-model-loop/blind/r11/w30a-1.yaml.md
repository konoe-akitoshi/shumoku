1. The format has no explicit way to say that AS64510 and AS64540 are transit providers, that AS64520 peering is settlement-free, or that the links represent eBGP sessions. I used connection IDs to group the session links, but their names are only labels by convention.
2. I added peer-router nodes for AS64520 and AS64530 to place their addresses in the IX-T LAN segment. The description gives their ASNs and addresses, but does not explicitly say those peers are routers. I also used IDs for the unnamed transit routers and peer nodes.
3. The format does not make clear how to represent a logical session that runs over a physical link: a `Connection` groups links but does not state their relationship. I represented the AS64510 session with its known cable link, the AS64520 session with both segment-attachment links and a logical node-to-node link, and the AS64540 session with a node-to-node link whose path is unknown.
