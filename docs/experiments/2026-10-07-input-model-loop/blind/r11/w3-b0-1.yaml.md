It also cannot represent the VMware port group as a distinct object; VLAN 10 stands in for it.
2. **Guesses/additions:** I used `server-room` as a group and the availability zones as groups. I treated `prod` as a redundancy set of the two hypervisors and used its ID for app-1’s unknown host. I inferred product paths, software names, and node types where the description identified the product or software but did not specify how to encode it. The two VPN tunnels are represented as virtual links; their endpoint ports and carried segments were not specified.
3. **Unclear in the format:** It says a node’s `network` applies to the node as a whole, while segment membership implies that network already. It is unclear whether a transit gateway attached through subnets should be represented as belonging to the VPC, as it is here, or by additional links to the subnet segments. The meaning and preferred values for `type`, and the normalization rules for `product` and `software`, are also unspecified.
