Add `layoutDrawing`, a new drawing layout that takes the network, its structure and the kit's metrics and returns a scene: heights from the flows and level pairs alone, frames as boxes around what they hold, sibling frames always stacked where the flows put one upstream of the other. Lines are wired in four steps: the free space is cut into cells and doors, each line takes its way through the doors, the lines at every door are put in the order that crosses least (changed along whole bundles), and every run of a line, and every port along its edge, is put on a coordinate by one solve for each axis, a pitch from the next, a lead-out from the cards, straight where it can be. Room that the wiring lacks (a card wide enough for its ports, a corridor wide enough for its lines) is asked of the placement, which only ever grows. Every end of a line stands at a port, named or not, and the lines that name one port of a box meet at one point of one side. Lines laid over the drawing ask for no room and are bundled in lanes of a band above it (virtual) or below it. What a virtual line leaves the network for (a space with no cable) stands above the cabled node it leaves, and what the structure gives no direction is reported as free. `sceneReport` and `wiringReport` measure a scene. The kit's drawing metrics gain `portSlot`.

The structure now also orients the cable-less vertices that no boundary point reaches, from the cabled vertices their virtual lines leave (each at distance 0): the cabled end is the nearer one. Lines between two cabled vertices still count on neither side.
