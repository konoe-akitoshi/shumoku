Space the ports along an edge by their own lines, not by the widest line on the box. Two
neighbouring ports keep the white and the ground of their two lines, as two lines side by side do;
before, one wide uplink spaced every other port on the box as if it were as wide, which widened the
box and pushed its ports out of line with the ports they join, bending lines that ran straight.
Drawings where every line is as wide as the others do not change.
