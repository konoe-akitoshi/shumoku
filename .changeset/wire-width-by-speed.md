Draw link lines by speed so the fast ones stand out: 1G at four strokes and seven and a half more
per decade (10G at 11.5, 25G at about 14.5, 100G at 19), never under two strokes. The earlier two
strokes and a half per decade (1G at 7.5, 100G at 12.5) drew the fastest lines barely thicker than
the commonest. A port's marker widens across a line wider than it, so a thick line ends in its port.
