2. **Guessed or added:** I used ASNs as the network name, inferred `router` types, and assigned IDs to unnamed peers. I placed the AS64520 and AS64530 peer nodes in group IX-T based on the description. I modeled the unknown AS64510 router and AS64540 peer as individual routers. The AS64510 cable and eBGP session are separate links grouped into one connection; I left the cable’s segments unknown. I marked the AS64540 link assumed because its physical path is unknown, though the session itself is stated to exist.
