1. The description’s statement that each cable carries VLAN 10 and VLAN 30 “and nothing else” is expressible because each link lists its complete set of segments. The format does not have a field to say that the VMs’ adapters use specific port groups; their segment membership and address are represented instead.
2. I added a `server-room` group and associated the switches, hosts, and VLAN segments with it, interpreting “server room” as a place. I also added routed network IDs for the staff and database prefixes, since the format models prefixes on routed networks and segments separately. `vm` is used as the VM node type.
3. The format does not say whether a `RoutedNetwork` should be created for every subnet, or whether a subnet’s `Segment.prefix` alone is sufficient. It also does not define standard values for `Node.type` or `Node.product`.
