1. The format has no explicit fields for an autonomous system number, BGP sessions, or commercial relationships such as customer/transit and settlement-free peering. I represented AS numbers as routing-domain IDs and described sessions and relationships in link descriptions.
2. I treated the AS645xx entries as routing domains and routers, and marked the provider routers as assumed nodes because their physical device identities are not confirmed. I modeled IX-T as a group and its peering LAN as a segment. I connected the known IX participants to that segment; the description says AS64530 is on the LAN, but does not identify its router, so its node is an assumed representative.
3. The format does not say whether a link to a segment should also list that segment in `segments`; I included it to state explicitly that the link carries the segment. It is also unclear whether an endpoint can stand for a whole AS or should always identify a specific router.
