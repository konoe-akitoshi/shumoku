Add `layoutDrawing`, a new drawing layout that takes the network, its structure and the kit's metrics and returns a scene: heights from the flows and level pairs alone, frames as boxes around what they hold, sibling frames always stacked where the flows put one upstream of the other. Lines are wired in four steps: the free space is cut into cells and doors, each line takes its way through the doors, the lines at every door are put in the order that crosses least (changed along whole bundles), and every run of a line, and every port along its edge, is put on a coordinate by one solve for each axis, a pitch from the next, a lead-out from the cards, straight where it can be. A run that goes through several doors is one passing in the order, the same at every door it goes through, and the orders are kept inside the room each run has. Room that the wiring lacks (a card wide and high enough for its ports, a corridor wide enough for its lines) is asked of the placement, which only ever grows, from the rooms that were too small and the items that bound them. Every end of a line stands at a port, named or not, and the lines that name one port of a box meet at one point of one side. A line leaves a box by the side its ends' relation gives it: a flow out of the bottom of the upper box and into the top of the lower, a line between level boxes by their sides, a line laid over the drawing by the side of its band; a port is a stretch of its side that the solve places, so a line that can go straight is straight, and a card is kept clear of by a lead-out only on the sides that have ports. Lines laid over the drawing ask for no room and are bundled in lanes of a band above it (virtual) or below it. What a virtual line leaves the network for (a space with no cable) stands above the cabled node it leaves, and what the structure gives no direction is reported as free. `drawingReport` measures a drawing: its size, crossings, wiring, ports and flow. The kit's drawing metrics gain `portSlot`.

The structure now also orients the cable-less vertices that no boundary point reaches, from the cabled vertices their virtual lines leave (each at distance 0): the cabled end is the nearer one. Lines between two cabled vertices still count on neither side.
