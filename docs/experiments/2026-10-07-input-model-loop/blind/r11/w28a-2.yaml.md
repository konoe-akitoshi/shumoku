1. The description does not specify physical links between br2 and IX-T’s peering LAN, or between the AS64520/AS64530 routers and that LAN. Those attachments are represented, but their physical paths are unknown.
2. I used `assumed: true` for the unnamed or unspecified remote routers because their existence is described only through their AS/address/session. I chose IDs for them. I treated AS64520 and AS64530’s addresses as router addresses.
3. The format does not have a field for commercial relationship (customer transit or settlement-free peering), or a distinct field for “eBGP session.” I put those details in link descriptions and marked session links `virtual: true`. It is unclear whether a session over an existing physical link should be represented as a separate virtual link, or as metadata on that physical link.
