1. The format cannot express the EC2 instance’s VPC instance-type field separately from its product identity, or explicitly model that the AWS VPN tunnels are IPsec.
2. I used conventional product paths and node types for the named products and services; the types and product paths are not prescribed by the format. I also named the main routing table `rt-1-main`, the guest VRF `rt-1-guest`, VLAN 10 `vlan-10`, and the VPN connection `rt-1-tgw-1-vpn`.
3. The format does not define conventions for `type`, `product`, or `software` values. It also does not specify how to represent a routing table separately from a routed network, or model which subnets a Transit Gateway attachment uses. I represented the two VPN tunnels as two links between the same endpoints; the format does not distinguish or name individual tunnels.
