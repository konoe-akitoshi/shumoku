Keep lines between two boxes side by side straight, and never let bent ones cross or run on each
other. Where the ports of two level boxes kept their middles from standing level while the lines
between them ran straight across, the lines were bent; now the boxes are let go of their middles
first, so the lines stay straight. Where lines between two boxes still have to bend, they turn in
the column in an order that crosses nothing, and the heights keep each landing clear of where the
next leaves, instead of crossing, or running on one another where two ports stood level.
