The drawing no longer stops on what the input asks for but cannot be drawn. A line the corridors give no way, a line that still closes a ring of separations after every relaxation, and a boundary the drawing layer names that is nothing on a line are left out or ignored, the rest is drawn, and each is named in the new `Scene.issues` (`DrawingIssue`). A failure of the engine itself still throws, now as `InvariantError` (`DrawingInvariantError` from the package root).

What is cabled to a redundancy set at the set's own height (the switch all its members use) now stands above the set instead of beside it, and of two sets cabled to each other one stands above the other: beside a set, only its two end members could be met, and a line to a member between them stopped the drawing with "no way through the corridors". A direction a cable gives also outranks a tie a virtual line leaves between the same two.
