1. **Could not express:** The cluster membership is represented by the `prod` redundancy set, and `app-1`’s unknown host is represented by `host: prod`. The format does not separately describe a vSphere cluster as a compute grouping. It also cannot express that the two VPN links are specifically IPsec tunnels.
2. **Guesses or additions:** I treated VLAN 10 as belonging to the main routing table and modeled it as a segment without a routing domain. I used product paths such as `amazon/ec2/t3.medium` and `amazon/rds/mysql`, and `ubuntu` / `amazon-linux` as software names. I marked the VPN links `assumed: true` because the specific tunnel endpoints are not described; the two links otherwise record the stated count and connection. The AWS region is reflected in the availability-zone group names.
3. **Unclear in the format:** `Redundancy` describes nodes that “stand in for one another,” which may not fully capture HA cluster membership where both hosts run workloads. The endpoint model does not identify tunnel-specific details, while `assumed` could mean the connection itself is uncertain rather than its particulars. The meaning of `RoutingDomain.prefix` for a routing domain that has subnets is also unclear.
