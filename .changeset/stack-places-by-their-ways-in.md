One line between two places that each have their own way in no longer puts all of one under all of the other. A place is stacked under another only when it hangs from it (every flow into it comes through the other) or when the flows already put every way into it below the other. A line into a box deep in a group drawn on its own no longer moves the whole group, its top included, down a rank.
