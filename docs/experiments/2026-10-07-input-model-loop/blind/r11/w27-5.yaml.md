1. The format has no field for autonomous system numbers as identifiers distinct from node or routing-domain IDs, nor for explicitly recording that a routing domain is an AS. It also cannot express the eBGP session details or the settlement-free terms as structured properties; I included them in connection labels.
2. The unnamed routers for AS64510, AS64520, AS64530, and AS64540 are represented by placeholder nodes. Their existence is implied by the description, but their names and router roles are not specified. I used placeholder IDs and marked them assumed. I marked `product: '?'` on all routers because their makers are unknown.
3. I modeled IX-T’s peering LAN as a segment and attached br2 to it. I modeled the AS64520 peering as a separate link carrying that segment, and AS64530 as present on the segment without a link to br2, consistent with the stated addresses and lack of a session. I represented AS64540’s unknown path as a virtual link because the format has no way to state that a link’s physical or logical nature is unknown; this is a guess. The format is also unclear about how to represent a node’s membership in an AS routing domain when no explicit attachment or segment is described, and how to distinguish a point-to-point transit link from a session carried over a shared LAN.
